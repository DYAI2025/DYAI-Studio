Work & Evidence

Claims should be inspectable.

AI makes it very easy to produce convincing demonstrations.

A demonstration is not the same as a working system.

DYAI separates different levels of evidence.

  1. Level 01

    CONCEPT

    A proposed idea or system.

  2. Level 02

    PROTOTYPE

    Built enough to test.

  3. Level 03

    USED IN PRACTICE

    Used in real work.

  4. Level 04

    REPEATED PATTERN

    Worked across more than one relevant situation.

  5. Level 05

    MEASURED CASE

    A meaningful outcome has been observed against an appropriate baseline.

We distinguish what is imagined, what is built, what has been used and what has actually been measured.

No prototype is presented as proof.

No estimate becomes ROI simply because it sounds plausible.

FAQ

Questions people ask before bringing a problem.

What is Augmented Intelligence?

At DYAI, Augmented Intelligence means designing systems in which artificial intelligence extends human capability without making human judgement, responsibility or context invisible.

The focus is not AI replacing people.

It is designing better ways for people and AI to work together.

What is an AI Practice?

AI Practice is DYAI's term for the repeatable way an organisation works with AI.

It can include AI literacy, workflow design, model and tool selection, human review, agents, governance, evaluation and organisational learning.

The objective is to move beyond isolated experiments toward a capability the organisation can actually use and improve.

How is DYAI different from traditional AI consulting?

DYAI does include advisory work when it is useful.

But advice alone is not the intended outcome.

We connect understanding, design, implementation, real-world use, verification and learning around a concrete work problem.

Does DYAI build AI agents?

Yes — when an agent is the right solution to a validated problem.

An agent can be useful when work requires multiple steps, access to tools or data, or coordinated capabilities.

We do not begin by assuming that every workflow needs an agent.

Can DYAI help teams work with ChatGPT, Claude and other LLMs?

Yes.

That may start with practical AI literacy: understanding how LLMs work, how to communicate with them, how context and instructions affect results, and where their limitations matter.

The aim is to connect that knowledge to real work rather than teach prompting in isolation.

Does DYAI train AI models?

“Training AI” can mean very different things.

Many useful business systems do not need a newly trained model.

Depending on the problem, the right approach may involve prompting, context design, retrieval, tools, integrations, evaluation, fine-tuning decisions or another architecture.

We choose that after understanding the requirement.

What is Personal Augmentation?

Personal Augmentation is DYAI's high-context approach for founders, independent professionals and creators.

It connects strategic thinking, AI capability, technical systems and everyday work around the individual's real goals and working environment.
