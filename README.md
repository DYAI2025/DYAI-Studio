# DYAI Studio

**Design Your Augmented Intelligence**

DYAI Studio is the project behind `studio.dyai.cloud`: a bilingual Augmented Intelligence Studio platform designed to make practical human–AI collaboration understandable, experiential and measurable.

## Product direction

DYAI is organised around three connected layers:

1. **DYAI for Organisations — AI Practice**: turn real organisational problems and workflows into working human–AI practices.
2. **DYAI Personal — Personal Augmentation**: high-context work for founders, independent professionals and creators whose strategy, tools, work and digital identity are tightly connected.
3. **DYAI Lab — Reusable IP**: convert repeated delivery patterns into reusable skills, agent patterns, evaluation methods, workflow templates, governance patterns and later productised software.

The website is not intended to be a brochure. Its product requirement is to move a relevant visitor from abstract AI curiosity to recognition of a real work problem, intuitive understanding of augmented intelligence, credible evidence and a useful next action.

## Working principles

- problem and outcome before tool;
- human agency over magic autonomy;
- evidence over unsupported claims;
- German/English parity by design;
- accessible and performant interaction;
- small end-to-end value slices;
- customer work should produce reusable learning and assets where evidence supports reuse.
